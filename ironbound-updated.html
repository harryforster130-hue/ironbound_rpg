How stats work

Strength reflects your logged weighted training volume and estimated best sets; Endurance reflects cardio and climbing entries; Agility reflects movement/climbing activity; Vitality reflects consistency. Stats are illustrative game scores, not medical or scientifically validated fitness measurements. They rise gradually from your activity history and are capped at 99.
